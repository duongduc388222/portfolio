HOBBIES.LOG

District golds and City medals in badminton back in Vietnam, a club singles title in the US, football on the weekend, and a steady rotation of strategy and puzzle games. Sports taught me discipline under physical pressure; games taught me decision-making under uncertainty. Both run as background processes while research runs in the foreground.
